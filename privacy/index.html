Privacy Policy

Big Panda Games · Effective October 22, 2026

This policy applies to all games published by Big Panda Games on Google Play and the App Store, including Transylvania Bubble Shooter. Our games are made for a general audience that includes children.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information. Our games have no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no way to share content with other users. Game progress and settings (for example level and sound preferences) are saved only on your device.

Advertising

Some games show ads served by Google AdMob. Because our audience includes children, every ad request is marked as child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent (GDPR), and ads are limited to the G content rating. In this mode:

  • ads are not personalized and are not based on interests or behavior;
  • the Android Advertising ID is not accessed;
  • AdMob may process limited technical data (such as IP address, device type and operating system) only to deliver ads, measure ads, and prevent fraud.

Google's handling of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in AdMob's child-directed treatment documentation.

Children's privacy

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR, and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Links

A game may open its own Google Play page so you can rate it. No information is passed to the store by us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
